## Brand & Style
The brand personality embodies curatorial reverence, precision, and scholarly luxury. Tailored for archivist-collectors, vinyl historians, and cataloguers of rare texts and recordings, the interface blends classical bibliographic cataloging with modern editorial clarity. The emotional target is scholarly composure: quiet, organized, authoritative, yet tactile and sensorial.

The design movement synthesizes Archival Editorial Minimalism with Sharp Institutional Structure. Surfaces mimic heavy warm-laid parchment and alabaster slips, framed with precise, razor-sharp geometric borders. Rather than clinical software tropes, it utilizes typographic rhythm, deep imperial amethyst ink tones, and lavender-cast archival storage trays. Visual clarity takes precedence; zero whimsical ornamentation or emojis are permitted. All visual symbols are rendered through crisp, stroke-accurate geometric SVG glyphs.

## Colors
The palette evokes century-old archival repositories, fine bookcloth bindings, and deep violet wax seals set against pressed alabaster vellum.

- **Brand Primaries:** `#3c096c` (Deep Royal Amethyst) anchors primary system interactions, primary actions, and navigational anchors. Its darker counterpart `#240046` (Imperial Obsidian Amethyst) commands major display titles and top-level navigational headers.
- **Accents & States:** `#5a189a` (Vibrant Amethyst) handles interactive hover states, catalog tags, and selection borders. `#7b2cbf` acts as an editorial accent for special archival indicators, timestamps, and matrix numbering.
- **Neutrals & Inks:** `#10002b` serves as the high-contrast master ink for all body typography, tables, and metadata keys, with `#1e1330` supporting dense secondary running text.
- **Backgrounds & Containers:** Base canvas rests on `#fbfaff` (Alabaster Linen). Secondary elevated containers, metadata plates, and card shells transition through `#f5f1fa` (Subtle Lavender Slip) down to `#eae3f4` (Pressed Archival Card). Dividers and structural rules are set in `#d6c7eb` (Sharp Lavender Rule) and `#bfa5e3` for active boundaries.

## Typography
Typographic discipline pairs the balanced geometric curves of Quicksand for display titles with the clinical, high-density legibility of Roboto Flex for dense discographic and bibliographic metadata.

- **Quicksand** is reserved for record titles, archival series identifiers, catalog headings, and edition numbers. Its distinctive geometry softens the rigidity of the archival framework without compromising legibility.
- **Roboto Flex** operates as the catalog engine: body passages, catalog indices, matrix runouts, and metadata tables use its variable metric flexibility to ensure effortless tracking across narrow columns, record labels, and archival ledger views.
- **Case & Tracking Strategy:** All category identifiers, catalog codes, and label tags (`label-sm`, `label-md`) must be styled with uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror archival physical stamping and library classification tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a strict 12-column architectural grid inspired by library folio catalog indexes. 

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) outer canvas margins. Elements align cleanly to structural bounding boxes to convey museum-grade organization.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) canvas margins. Multi-pane archival views fold secondary catalog indexes into collapsible drawer panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) margin. Grids collapse into vertical index slips; tabular metadata wraps into stacked key-value blocks.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Vertical rhythms rely entirely on the 8px baseline scale. Content grouping strictly observes layout spacing: tight catalog pairings use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, component padding leverages `space-md`, and structural index cards rely on `space-lg` to `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation rejects fuzzy, diffused commercial drop shadows in favor of crisp archival layering: structural borders, solid tonal slips, and high-precision hairline outlines.

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation is primarily achieved through contrasting surface values. Base floor (`#fbfaff`) supports middle-tier archival trays (`#f5f1fa`), which in turn support high-priority active inspection cards (`#ffffff`).
- **Archival Inset and Shadowing:** When floating dialogs or archival zoom trays require spatial detachment, elevation is articulated through a razor-edge hairline border (`1px solid #3c096c`) accompanied by a subtle, low-spread architectural contact shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(36, 0, 70, 0.04), 0 8px 16px rgba(36, 0, 70, 0.08)`.
- **Structural Outlines:** Panels, search rails, and record sleeves prioritize 1px border articulation in `#d6c7eb` rather than elevation lift. Focus and active selections reinforce depth by switching border values from `#d6c7eb` to deep `#3c096c`.

## Shapes
A pure sharp aesthetic (`roundedness: 0`) is applied universally across the system. 

- All containers, media sleeves, buttons, tabs, input borders, and badge chips utilize 0px corner radiuses.
- This clean, orthogonal profile mirrors vintage card catalogues, vinyl LP outer sleeves, folio folios, and technical index markers.
- Precision cut lines provide a scholarly discipline that distinguishes the platform from generic rounded modern applications, ensuring every record and volume appears held within a tangible archival frame.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in deep `#3c096c`, text in `#fbfaff`, 0px border-radius, 1px border in `#240046`. Hover transitions to `#5a189a`. Active state deepens to `#240046`. Text uses `label-lg` uppercase.
- **Secondary / Archival Outline:** Surface in `#fbfaff`, text in `#3c096c`, with a crisp 1px border in `#3c096c`. Hover shifts background to `#f5f1fa` with text in `#240046`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or fill; `#3c096c` text with underline appearing on hover.
- **Icon Buttons:** Square 36px or 44px containers, 1px border in `#d6c7eb`, housing 18px pure SVG monochrome vectors.

### Chips & Index Tags
- Used for shelf numbers, vinyl RPM, matrix codes, and preservation statuses.
- Square-edged (`0px` radius), padding `2px 8px`, border `1px solid #d6c7eb`.
- Background set to `#f5f1fa` with uppercase `label-sm` text in `#3c096c`. Status tags (e.g., "Archived", "Mint", "Restoration") utilize deep `#240046` borders.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background in `#ffffff` framed by a 1px solid `#d6c7eb` border. 
- Focus state instantly snaps to `1px solid #3c096c` with no outer glow rings.
- Text rendered in `#10002b` with placeholder text in `#7b2cbf` at 60% opacity.
- Prefix and suffix icons are monochrome SVGs tinted to `#3c096c`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Strict 0px squared inputs (radio buttons are distinctively styled as nested diamond or square indices to maintain the archival motif).
- Default: `#ffffff` surface with a 1.5px border in `#3c096c`.
- Checked: `#3c096c` fill with a crisp white SVG checkmark; radio uses a centered sharp inner square in `#3c096c`.

### Lists & Ledger Tables
- Alternating rows tinted softly between `#fbfaff` and `#f5f1fa`.
- Row dividers consist of 1px solid lines in `#eae3f4`.
- Column headers styled in `label-md`, uppercase, set in `#3c096c` with bottom border `2px solid #3c096c`.
- Hover row state shifts to `#eae3f4` with left indicator line accent in `#5a189a`.

### Cards & Archival Sleeves
- **Vinyl Sleeve Card:** Square aspect-ratio container with 1px border in `#d6c7eb`, `#ffffff` slip, with high-contrast text block below.
- **Bibliotheca Ledger Card:** Features a top header panel in `#f5f1fa` housing catalog numbers, followed by an alabaster `#fbfaff` body detailing volume, condition, and edition specifics, outlined in a 1px solid `#d6c7eb` shell.

### Specialized Archival Components
- **Matrix Runout Badge:** High-density mono-like data slip set in `Roboto Flex` `label-sm` with a dashed 1px border in `#7b2cbf`.
- **Faceted Drawer Filter:** Accordion filter lists framed with sharp lateral dividers, featuring SVG chevron toggles and quantity counts enclosed in brackets.
